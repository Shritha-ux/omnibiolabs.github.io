Mandatory Compliance Charter

OmniBio Safety Rules & Chapter Regulations

REGULATION 01 • BSL-1 & ZERO PATHOGENS

Approved Organisms Only

Only baker's yeast (S. cerevisiae), food-grade plants, or certified non-pathogenic E. coli K-12 strains are permitted. No human cheek swabs or wild pathogen culturing. Petri dishes are taped shut and never re-opened.

REGULATION 02 • PPE & CHEMICAL CAPS

Mandatory Goggles, Gloves & Low Concentrations

Splash goggles and nitrile gloves are required for all wet-labs. Hydrogen peroxide is strictly capped at 3% household concentration, ethidium bromide is banned (non-toxic food dyes only), and alcohol solvents require ventilation with zero open flames.

REGULATION 03 • HARDWARE & BIO-DISPOSAL

Low-Voltage & 10% Bleach Protocol

Electrophoresis rigs operate strictly at 18V–36V DC. Plastic slides replace glass to prevent cuts. Used microbial plates are submerged in 10% bleach prior to disposal, and raw egg/dairy/plant allergy screenings are completed before every session.

REGULATION 04 • MENTOR-TO-STUDENT RATIO

1:6 High School Mentor Supervision

Chapters must maintain at least 1 trained high school mentor per 6 K–8 students, plus an on-site teacher, librarian, or adult coordinator present throughout every session.

REGULATION 05 • ZERO-COST ACCESS

100% Free to K–8 Participants

Chapters may never charge K–8 students or public Title I schools for workshops. All reagents and hardware are funded via HCB 501(c)(3) grants, sponsors, and community donations.

REGULATION 06 • PRE-LAB SAFETY BRIEFING

Mandatory 5-Minute Safety Check

Every session begins with an allergy check (latex balloons, dairy/lactase, plant leaves, food dyes), PPE inspection, and vertical micropipette/tip-ejection safety rules before touching reagents.
