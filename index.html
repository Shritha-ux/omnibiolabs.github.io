Our Story

Closing the gap between textbooks and real biotech.

Founded by student scientists in Mountain House, California, OmniBio Labs started with a simple observation: most students don't touch a micropipette or run a DNA gel until late high school or college—long after many decide science "isn't for them."

We built OmniBio Labs as a student-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit to change that early. By bringing mobile wet-lab equipment directly into K–8 classrooms at zero cost, we give younger students the thrill of doing real science alongside approachable student mentors.

501(c)(3) TRANSPARENCY & SAFETY

Safe reagents, supervised labs, public accountability.

Every OmniBio protocol uses food-grade, non-toxic, biosafety-level-1-exempt materials designed specifically for K–8 classrooms. All student mentors complete safety training prior to visiting partner schools.
