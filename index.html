    How a visit works, and how we keep it safe

    A visit, start to finish

    1. Plan. Pick labs and a date with your teacher, and tell us about allergies or other needs.
    2. Set up. Mentors arrive, set up the tables and check every material.
    3. Guess, then test. Small groups predict what will happen, then run each step themselves.
    4. Wrap up. Students share what they saw, and mentors clean up.

    Our safety rules

    • No lab runs without the school’s approval and an adult from the school in the room.
    • We share the materials list and safety notes with the teacher before every visit.
    • Goggles and gloves for every student when a lab calls for them.
    • Mentors handle anything hot, flammable or electrical.
    • We check for allergies before any lab that uses food or milk products.
    • We never share student photos, names or work without written permission.

    Quick answers

    Is it really free for schools?

    Yes. Workshops cost schools nothing. We are funded by donations through HCB.

    Who teaches the labs?

    High school student mentors lead each table, and an adult from the school stays in the room.

    Which grades do you serve?

    K–8. Every lab lists its grade range, and you can book by grade band. We confirm the best fit with your teacher.

    What does a teacher need to do?

    Offer a classroom with tables, a time slot and an adult in the room. We confirm everything else, such as sinks, allergies and setup space, when we plan the visit.

    Are the labs safe?

    Each lab is built around classroom-safe materials, and mentors handle anything hot, flammable or electrical. Every lab has a safety note you can read first. See our safety rules above.

    How do the labs fit our science standards?

    We do not claim formal standards alignment yet. Tell us your grade and unit, and we will help you choose a lab that fits.

    Where do you teach?

    We started in Mountain House, California. Tell us where your school is and we will see what is possible, or start a chapter near you.

    Where does my donation go?

    HCB keeps a 7% fiscal sponsorship fee. The rest goes to OmniBio Labs for lab supplies and workshop costs. Use the slider above for an estimate.

    Will my students’ photos be shared?

    No. We never share student photos, names or work without written permission from a parent, guardian or the school.

    Is OmniBio Labs a real nonprofit?

    We are a fiscally sponsored project of The Hack Foundation (d.b.a. Hack Club), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. HCB handles donations and receipts.
