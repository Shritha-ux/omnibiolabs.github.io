Mandatory Compliance Charter

OmniBio Safety Rules & Regulations

Built specifically for elementary and middle school classroom safety approval.

RULE 01 • BSL-1 & ZERO PATHOGENS

Approved Organisms Only

Only baker's yeast (S. cerevisiae), food-grade plants, or certified non-pathogenic E. coli K-12 strains are allowed. Zero human cheek swabs or wild pathogen culturing. Petri dishes are taped shut and never re-opened.

RULE 02 • PPE & CHEMICAL CAPS

Mandatory Goggles, Gloves & Low Caps

Splash goggles and nitrile gloves are mandatory. Hydrogen peroxide is capped at 3% household grade, ethidium bromide is banned (food dyes only), and alcohol requires ventilation with zero flames.

RULE 03 • HARDWARE & BIO-DISPOSAL

Low-Voltage & 10% Bleach Disposal

Electrophoresis rigs run strictly at 18V–36V DC batteries. Plastic slides replace glass. Used microbial plates are submerged in 10% bleach for 20 minutes before disposal.

RULE 04 • SUPERVISION RATIO

1:6 High School Mentor Ratio

Every session requires at least 1 trained high school mentor per 6 K–8 students, plus an on-site school teacher or librarian present at all times.

RULE 05 • ZERO-COST ACCESS

100% Free to K–8 Participants

Chapters never charge students or public schools. Supplies are funded via HCB 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsorship, PTA grants, and community sponsors.

RULE 06 • ALLERGY & SAFETY CHECK

Mandatory 5-Minute Briefing

Allergy checks (latex balloons, dairy/lactase, plant leaves, raw eggs) and micropipette vertical-handling rules are reviewed before opening any kit.
