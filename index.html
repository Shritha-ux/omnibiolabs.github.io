Our Story & Fiscal Transparency

Why wait until college to hold a micropipette?

Founded by high school students in Mountain House, California, OmniBio Labs started with a simple observation: elementary and middle school students love real science, yet schools rarely have the budget or lab gear to move beyond textbook worksheets.

We bridge that gap by bringing sanitized, classroom-safe biotechnology instruments directly into K–8 classrooms—completely free of charge—taught by trained student mentors who make science approachable, safe, and genuinely fun.

501(c)(3) VIA HCB

OmniBio Labs operates under the fiscal sponsorship of HCB (The Hack Foundation), ensuring full financial transparency, tax-deductible receipts for donors, and strict classroom safety standards.

PEACEFUL, INCLUSIVE LEARNING

Every lab is designed so shy learners, visual thinkers, and future scientists alike get their own hands-on tools—never watching from the back of the room.
